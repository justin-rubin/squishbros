Squish Bros Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026

Squish Bros is published by SELLCURB LLC. The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking software.
  • The app has no in-app purchases.
  • The app does not connect to the internet to play.
  • Game progress is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes that progress.

Because the app collects no information, we have none to share, sell or delete on request.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
