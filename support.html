Squish Bros Support

Need help with Squish Bros? Email support@sellcurb.com and we will get back to you.

My progress is gone

Progress is stored only on the device. It does not move between devices, and deleting the app erases it.

How do I get the Snack Pack?

Buy the Snack Pack add-on on the Upgrades screen with Bro Points earned in the game. It costs no real money.

Does the game cost anything?

No. It is free, with no ads and no purchases.
